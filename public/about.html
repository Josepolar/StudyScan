About StudyScan

StudyScan is an independent school learning application developed by Josepolar. It brings teacher-provided lessons, assignments, feedback, and AI-assisted explanations into one classroom workspace.

StudyScan is not a Google product and does not represent Google. Teachers provide and approve learning material; the AI supports learning and does not replace a teacher.

How account access works

You can create a StudyScan account with an email address and a separate StudyScan password. Google sign-in instead takes you to accounts.google.com, with the authentication callback handled by Supabase. StudyScan never asks you to enter your Google password into its own form.

New accounts receive Student access. Only the school administrator can invite teachers. A matching Google sign-in and explicit invitation acceptance are required before Teacher access is granted. Students join classes using teacher-provided codes and see their assigned materials.

AI assistance

The assistant uses Groq to explain relevant teacher-provided material. School and teacher controls determine when it is available. Responses include source passages when supported. Answers can be incorrect; ask your teacher when uncertain. Free provider limits can make the assistant temporarily unavailable.

Questions or concerns

Contact your teacher or school administrator about class access, learning records, or AI permission. Developer information is available on Josepolar's portfolio, and the project source is on GitHub.
