Data & privacy

This notice describes StudyScan's current data flow. Your school must also explain its own authorization, retention, and access procedures before learners use the service.

Account and classroom information

StudyScan stores your account identifier, name, email address, role, and account status. Classroom records can include class membership, learning materials and attachments, assignments, submissions, feedback, progress, assistant questions and answers, and teacher follow-up notes.

Sign-in

Supabase handles authentication. Email sign-in uses your StudyScan account password. Google sign-in requests basic profile and email access; it does not request access to your Gmail messages. Enter a Google password only on Google's own sign-in page, never in StudyScan's email sign-in form.

Who can access learning records

Students can access their assigned materials and personal work. Classroom teachers can review the questions, AI responses, submissions, and progress available in their classes. Super Admin controls manage school accounts and settings; the administration dashboard shows usage counts rather than student conversation contents. Authorized service operators administer the underlying infrastructure.

AI processing

When you ask the assistant, the server sends your question, selected relevant lesson text, and limited conversation context to Groq for generation and a safety check. Account names and email addresses are not deliberately added to that AI request, but personal details included in a question or lesson could still be sent. Keep personal, medical, financial, and other sensitive information out of questions and learning materials.

The school must authorize learner AI use, and teachers must authorize eligible students before student requests are enabled. Ask your teacher for direct help if AI access is unavailable.

Service providers

Vercel hosts the website. Supabase provides authentication, database storage, private learning resource storage, and server functions. Groq provides AI processing. These services also process operational information needed to deliver their services. See Vercel, Supabase, Groq, and Google for their respective privacy information.

Questions, corrections, and deletion requests

Contact your teacher or school administrator about your records, corrections, deletion, or withdrawal of AI permission. Retention and deletion are not currently automatic; the school must arrange an appropriate process with the service operator. Developer information is available through Josepolar's portfolio.
